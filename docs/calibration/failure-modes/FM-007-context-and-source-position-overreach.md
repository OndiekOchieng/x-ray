# FM-007 — Context and source-position overreach

A legal graph can still overstate what its sources establish. This v0.3 failure mode is evaluated through typed, model-assisted Reviewer judgments, never by source rank or keyword rules in the runtime.

## Detection

Compare each material claim and finding with the evidence proposition, knowledge basis, source position, time scope, and related claims. Flag a promotion only when the broader proposition lacks its own support.

## Repair

Narrow the claim/finding or trace evidence for the broader proposition. Retain the prior review finding and route a blocking judgment to the responsible research stage.
