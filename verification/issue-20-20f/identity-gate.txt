=== the rule as implemented ===
same normalised locator + same STAGE-COMPUTED content hash, in one run
  = one canonical Source

  locator differs                  -> two Sources (never collapsed on hash alone)
  hash differs, same locator       -> two Sources (a changed record is a new observation)
  either hash missing              -> two Sources (identity is established, never assumed)

Two decisions turn on it, and they answer different questions:
  sameArtifact()      "have I already got this?"   -> minting: reuse the id
  isSurfaceArtifact() "is this the article under investigation?" -> withhold it
                                                      from a SURFACE claim

Withholding consults the surface document in hand AND the graph's surface
Source, so it keeps working on a resumed run where the in-memory material is
empty. The XR-INV-001 id comparison is the third layer.

=== why this was an invariant defect, not a tidiness one ===
validateEpistemics: `if (e.sourceId !== surfaceSourceId) continue`

First light had surfaceSourceId=SRC-001 and evidence citing SRC-006 — the same
artifact under a second id. The duplicate LAUNDERED the surface record past a
DETERMINISTIC invariant, and the article became evidence for a claim decomposed
out of itself with validation unable to see it.

One artifact, one id, is what makes that comparison sound. Nothing in the
validator changed.
